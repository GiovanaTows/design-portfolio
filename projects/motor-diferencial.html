Overview

The Differential Motor is an ongoing engineering project exploring a new way to convert fuel into energy, based on what the client calls the differential thermodynamic cycle. A smaller-scale prototype was already running at the time, but the project needed funding to build a larger motor that could prove its efficiency.

I was asked to design a short presentation explaining how the motor's cycle works and the different situations it could be applied to, from power generation to space propulsion.

Presentation

Nine slides, illustrated from scratch: how the differential cycle compares to classic engine cycles, how its conversion system works, and where it could replace existing technology — from steam turbines and Stirling engines to rocket propulsion and submarine power.
