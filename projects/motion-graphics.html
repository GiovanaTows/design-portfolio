Overview

Between 2019 and 2020, I worked as a freelance motion designer, creating animated graphics for a handful of clients across very different industries — a US ports platform, a US logistics company, a Brazilian construction materials brand, and two Latin American nutrition brands.

Some of these pieces were built to explain a product on a website, breaking down how a service works in a way plain text or static screens couldn't. Others were shorter, punchier animations made for social media, meant to grab attention in a feed and reinforce each brand's identity.

For each project, I illustrated the scenes in Adobe Illustrator and animated them in After Effects, working from the client's brief and existing brand guidelines.

Port Pass

Port Pass is a US ports service. In 2019, I animated a set of explainers for their website showing how their freeflow system gives trucking fleets a faster, more visible way to move containers in and out of port.

Loadsmart

Loadsmart is a US logistics company. In 2020, I animated an explainer for their website showing how their platform automatically matches a shipment to the best carrier bid, taking the guesswork out of booking freight.

You can also see this animation as a Lottie file at lottiefiles.com.

Balaroti

Balaroti is a Brazilian construction materials retailer. I created a series of motion graphics for their social media channels.

Age Nutrilatina

Age Nutrilatina is a Latin American supplements brand. I created a series of motion graphics for their social media channels.

Prodiet

Prodiet is a Latin American medical nutrition brand. I created a series of motion graphics for their social media channels.
