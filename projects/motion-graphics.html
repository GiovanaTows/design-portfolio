Overview

Between March 2017 and November 2018, I worked as a motion designer at the Brazilian agency Betminds, creating animated graphics for a very diverse range of clients.

Some of these pieces were built to explain a product or a service, others were shorter, punchier animations made for social media, meant to grab attention in a feed and reinforce each brand's identity.

For each project, I illustrated the scenes in Adobe Illustrator and animated them in After Effects, working from the client's brief and existing brand guidelines. After leaving the agency, I made a few other motion graphics projects as a freelancer.

Below I present a few examples of my motion graphics works, including some of the pieces I created for Loadsmart, Balaroti, Age Nutrilatina, Regenerate Pro, and Prodiet.

Loadsmart — Dynamic Freight Pricing

Role
Motion Designer / Illustrator
Tools
After Effects / Illustrator
Timeline
Jun 26 – Jul 1, 2020

Loadsmart is a US logistics company. They asked me to create an animation explaining their Dynamic Freight Pricing service.

I illustrated and animated the piece, showing how a shipment is matched to the best carrier bid automatically, in a simple, easy-to-follow way. I also exported it as a Lottie file to be used on their website, which allows for a smaller file size and better performance than a video.

The animation is still live on their website (2026).

Loadsmart — PortPass

Role
Motion Designer / Illustrator
Tools
After Effects / Illustrator
Timeline
Nov 8 – Dec 5, 2020
(40 hours)

Loadsmart also asked me to create three animations introducing a new service they were developing: PortPass. These animations would be applied in a carousel with text at the top of a landing page.

I first created the digital illustrations using references and color schemes provided by the client, then animated them in Adobe After Effects.

PortPass idea was to provide trucking fleets with a faster, more efficient way to move through the port.

I illustrated two of its features: ExpressGrid fast-tracks a fleet's way in and out of the port with expedited access; And FreeFlow reserves a stack of containers exclusively for the fleet's trucks.
