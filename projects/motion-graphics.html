Overview

Between 2019 and 2020, I worked as a freelance motion designer, creating animated graphics for a handful of clients across very different industries — a US ports platform, a US logistics company, a Brazilian construction materials brand, and two Latin American nutrition brands.

Some of these pieces were built to explain a product on a website, breaking down how a service works in a way plain text or static screens couldn't. Others were shorter, punchier animations made for social media, meant to grab attention in a feed and reinforce each brand's identity.

For each project, I illustrated the scenes in Adobe Illustrator and animated them in After Effects, working from the client's brief and existing brand guidelines.

Loadsmart — Dynamic Freight Pricing

Role
Motion Designer / Illustrator
Tools
After Effects / Illustrator
Timeline
Jun 26 – Jul 1, 2020

Loadsmart is a US logistics company. They contacted me asking for an animation to explain their Dynamic Freight Pricing service.

I made the digital illustration and its motion graphics animation. The animation shows how a shipment is matched to the best carrier bid automatically in a very simple and didatical way.

The animation is still live on their website (2026).

Loadsmart — PortPass

Role
Motion Designer / Illustrator
Tools
After Effects / Illustrator
Timeline
Nov 8 – Dec 5, 2020
(40 hours)

Loadsmart also asked me to create three animations introducing a new service they were developing, PortPass, for a carousel at the top of a webpage.

I first created the digital illustrations using references and color schemes provided by the client, then animated them in Adobe After Effects.

PortPass gives trucking fleets a faster, more efficient way to move through the port, promising full control and visibility with at least double the efficiency of the traditional process.

I illustrated two of its features: ExpressGrid and FreeFlow.

ExpressGrid fast-tracks a fleet's way in and out of the port with expedited access.

FreeFlow reserves a stack of containers exclusively for the fleet's trucks.
