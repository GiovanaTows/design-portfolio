Civi Protege — Premium plan

One of the last major features we shipped at Civi was a paid premium plan, "Civi Protege," which let only premium users add contacts.

Free users got alerts close to themselves and their saved places. Premium users could also see their contacts' current location and location history, battery level, and get notified whenever a contact arrived at or left a specific place — home, office, or school, for example.

We also designed a free trial flow so free users could try the paid features for two weeks.
