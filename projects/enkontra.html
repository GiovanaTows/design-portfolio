Enkontra.com Website

A concept for a long-term housing rental website, built for Brazil

Role
UI/UX Designer
Tools

Team
GRPCOM contacted the agency I worked for to redesign Enkontra.com, focusing on long-term rental contracts. The team included Rafael Ono Pasquini (Project Manager), Vinicius Manzano (UX Designer), and me as UI Designer.

Overview

Enkontra.com launched in October 2014 as a classifieds marketplace by Grupo Paranaense de Comunicação (GRPCOM), where people could buy and sell houses, apartments, cars, electronics, and more, combining listings from Gazeta do Povo, Tribuna, and Paraná Online. Read more about its launch on Gazeta do Povo.

The proposal for this new interface was "hassle-free rental". Unlike Airbnb, the site works around long-term contracts, and the main goal was to simplify the rental process as much as possible, cutting down on paperwork and bureaucracy.

Together, we designed the full flow — a landing page built to convert, a search experience with map and filters, a property page detailed enough to rent sight-unseen, a guided multi-step rental process, and a panel for owners to manage their listings and talk to prospective tenants — generating more than 40 screens in total.

Landing page

The landing page leads with "Your dream home, however and wherever you need it", a search bar that lets people search by tags — such as family, pool, countryside, rustic furniture — instead of rigid filters, and a set of featured listings by neighborhood.

After

Search & filters

Results can be refined by area, price, and minimum stay, or browsed directly on a map — useful for renters who care more about location than any single spec.

After

Property page

Each listing breaks the property down room by room — bedrooms, kitchen, bathrooms, living room — with its own photos and details, plus a chat where renters can ask the owner questions directly.

Rental process

Renting a property is a guided, six-step flow. This step lets the renter pick their move-in and move-out dates against the owner's minimum stay, then choose between paying month to month or paying the full period upfront for a discount.

After

Owner panel

Owners get their own management panel to track views and favorites on each listing, and reply to renter questions right from their proposals.
